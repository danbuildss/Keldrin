# Keldrin brand (unchanged; approved 2026-11-27, kept 2026-11-28)

This records the brand identity that is already live. The founder said to keep it ("the one we already have leave it").
Positioning and messaging are in POSITIONING.md.

- **Name:** always lowercase "keldrin" in public copy (website, README, agent guide, MCP, listings, posts), including
  at the start of a sentence (founder, 2026-11-28: "small letter K"). The wordmark is Inter 700 with tight letter spacing.
- **Logo:** "keystone" mark (logo A), a trapezoid with a vertical slot, in Base blue.
- **Colours:** Base blue #0000FF; near-black #111111; white #FFFFFF; greys #F8F8F8, #DDE3E9, #787878; tint #D1D7FF.
  Status colours: good #16a34a, warn #b45309, bad #b91c1c.
- **Type:** Inter for text; JetBrains Mono for labels, data and code.
- **Style:** square corners, thin lines, a dotted-grid motif and short reveal animations (reduced-motion respected).
  Numbers on the site come only from the live API.
- **Footer:** "keldrin, built by SOMEHOW", with SOMEHOW linking to https://somehowinternet.app/.
- **Not used:** the Base logo or font (keldrin is not affiliated with Base, Coinbase or Chainlink), and the
  Amber/Modular K identity (declined 2026-11-28).
