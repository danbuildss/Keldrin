# Quality and metrics
Build gold set of at least 10 manually reviewed real historical events across initial types, including amended filings, future vs effective actions, duplicate news, ticker/class ambiguity, missing feed and multiplier change. Record expected fields and evidence. Unit tests: identity, schema, timestamps, dedup, source conflict, correction, stale status, missing values, price semantics, replay and payment error handling. No unsupported return calculations.

Proposed launch gates: 100% source linkage and valid schema for published material facts; zero known critical identity/multiplier errors in gold set; independent review on each published event; at least 3 successful manual or controlled runs before recurring schedule. Report denominators and failure cases. Monitor precision, event coverage against sampled filings, time-to-detection, verification lag, stale rate, uptime and cost per successful record. No claim of completeness or real-time coverage without measurement.

Adoption: distinct external builders, actual integration calls, repeat consumers, paid successful requests and verifiable transaction hashes (privacy-safe). Separate founder tests and demos from genuine external usage. No artificial volume, wash activity or fabricated metrics. Scorecard in memory/scorecard.md. Improvements proposed through bounded PRs requiring owner approval.
