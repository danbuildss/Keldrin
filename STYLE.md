# Style
Clear, concise financial research prose. Lead with verified facts, then relevant implications and limitations. Use dates, units and source links. Avoid hype, ungrounded causality, guarantees, trading recommendations, engagement bait and artificial certainty. Draft social posts in founder-approved natural voice; never invent first-person experience. Public outputs distinguish announced, scheduled, effective, observed and interpreted.

Use Soundshuman if approved/available; draft → fact check → voice edit → proof → founder approval. No promotional claims, fabricated metrics, or rewritten factual meaning.
