## Found and fixed in this audit (PR #34)
1. **Phone menu:** nav links were hidden on phones on the home page (reported by the founder). They now sit behind a Menu button on every page.
2. **Playground:** it was 107 px wider than a phone screen (long endpoint names in the dropdown). It now fits.
3. **Explorer:** pages for tokens without a NAV feed asked for NAV and risk data and logged two harmless 404 errors. They now request only what the token's coverage says exists.

## Known, not broken (open items)
- **Robot timing:** GitHub skipped or delayed scheduled runs (1 of 2 ran, 3 h 41 min late). The fix is ready: cron-job.org, waiting on the founder (T060).
- **Earnings:** live, but empty until the first research run after #33 finishes. The first backfill can take 1–2 runs (600 checks per run).
- **Verifier login (ROADMAP 1.5):** the verifier should have its own database login. Waiting on the founder.
- **Failed SEC company lookups** are retried every run. This costs a few requests and is not a correctness issue.
- **NVDAc NAV:** the late-November drop (about −47% from the October peak) is real Chainlink data, so the risk numbers reflect it.
- **Users:** no confirmed external users, integrations or revenue yet.
