The proof behind every answer

Every value names where it came from.

01

Chainlink round ids

Each NAV point carries its feed, phase, aggregator and round id, so anyone can re-read it with getRoundData.

npx keldrin verify-nav NVDAc
02

SEC filings linked

Dividends and splits are matched to the issuer's filing and the onchain multiplier change before they are served.

GET /v1/assets/{token}/corporate-actions
03

Onchain, at one block

DEX prices are read at a single pinned block from verified pools, with depth capped by what each pool really holds.

npx keldrin verify-dex NVDAc
