How keldrin verifies
  • Corporate actions: each record is matched to the issuer's SEC filing and the onchain multiplier event, then re-checked by an independent verifier before it is served.
  • Chainlink NAV: every round is re-read with getRoundData through a second, independent RPC provider; only rounds the proxy officially served are used.
  • DEX pools: re-derived from the canonical factories (and the v4 pool id from its key); depth is capped by what each pool holds.
  • Live values (NAV, DEX price) are read onchain at request time; if a read fails, the answer says "unknown" with a reason, never a guess.
Freshness and limits
  • Staleness is judged in NYSE trading time against each feed's own verified update rhythm: fresh, expected_closed, unexpectedly_stale or unknown.
  • Research runs every 8 hours on GitHub Actions (GitHub may delay a run).
  • NAV history starts at each feed's first official round (August 2026; ANTc November 2026).
  • The NAV is Chainlink's published reference, not a live equity price. Facts with provenance, not advice.
